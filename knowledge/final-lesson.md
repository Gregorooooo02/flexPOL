# Ready for the real world?

Six closed questions bring the course together: **age group, goal, time horizon, financial readiness, ability to wait after losses, and comfort with volatility**. The readiness question combines the emergency buffer, expensive debt and whether the money is genuinely spare. Access needs and a loss's impact are covered by the ability-to-wait question.

The learner sees one proposed category, a short reason, its main trade-off and a four-step arrow flow. For example:

**Broad-market index fund / ETF → compare providers → check risks and fees → open an account if appropriate → start affordably and review.**

On small screens, the same flow runs vertically with down arrows. There is no separate introduction, additional reflection question or downloadable checklist. Finishing the lesson never requires a purchase.

## Content contract

- `final-lesson.json` supplies the six questions, ordered rules, category descriptions and visual next steps. It is imported directly by the frontend. This Markdown file documents the activity rather than adding a database chapter.
- The first matching rule wins: guardian support → financial foundations → missing information → access/capital preservation → cautious comparison → long-term diversification. Unknown capacity inputs never default to a growth result. The result shows at most two short reasons.
- Age handles the minor-account boundary and retirement context, not an automatic investment allocation. Retirement adds a short note about pension accounts. The activity points to the learner's local regulator without collecting their country.
- Results suggest categories to explore, not named products, allocation percentages, expected returns or a regulated suitability determination. Six questions cannot fully assess someone's finances or whether their goal is affordable. Local taxes, eligibility, costs, risks and protection still need checking.
- Responses and results remain only in page memory, never in storage, API requests or AI coach context. Closing the activity or refreshing clears them.
- The activity remains the final learning-path node after Chapter 3's test. Completion uses the existing in-memory progress, with no extra quiz, backend attempt or real-money action.
